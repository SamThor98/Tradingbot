              Settings logic guide
              what each control changes
            
            
    
              
      
                
    • Onboarding: connect account (orders) and market data (quotes), verify tokens, then test with a scan or paper order before going live.
      • 
                
    • Presets: profile picks a fixed risk template; apply shows a before/after preview, then persists it. Mode toggles plain vs raw values; rank explain sets tooltip vs inline rationale.
      • 
                
    • Guardrails (enforced per preset): Conservative caps a position at $300 and three live trades per day; Balanced at $500 and five; Aggressive at $900 and eight. Conservative runs hard quality gates and blocks event risk; aggressive shadows event and execution checks instead of blocking.
      • 
                
    • Screener thesis: trend-continuation breakouts (uptrend + volatility pattern), fast first-pass filter, deeper follow-up analysis, market regime checks, and visible near-misses with reasons.
      • 
                
    • Live trading: enabling requires risk acknowledgment plus typing ENABLE in the confirm box; pause blocks new approvals; 2FA protects sensitive actions; automation stays off until you opt in.
      • 
              
    
              
    
                Full risk and legal context: legal & risk disclosures.
                Connection help: Schwab connect guide.

                Website feature guide
                
        
                
          Five workspaces: Today (scan → review → approve), Research (portfolio and a name),
          Intel (open positions), System (health, calibration, and connection),
          and Backtest (single-window replays). Settings live inside System.
        
                
        
                  
          
                • Today: one tradeable name at a time. Queue only when analog and filings are clear.
                  • 
          
                • Research: equity weight, stops, quick check, dossier, SEC compare.
                  • 
          
                • Intel: conviction and options on names you already hold.
                  • 
          
                • System: health, the labeled profit-factor board, Schwab connect, and risk presets.
                  • 
          
                • Backtest: one window at a time. A single-window profit factor is not the signed book.
                  • 
        
                
        
                
          For per-setting behavior, open System → Risk presets.
